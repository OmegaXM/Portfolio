Featured project

Weave: one connected home for onboarding and offboarding

A People & Culture team was running the employee lifecycle through a form, emails and memory. There was no review step, no access control and no idea how any of it would behave at scale. I built Weave to replace that with one system, and then tried to break it.

What it does

  • New-hire intake lands in a pending review queue, so nothing starts without a human decision.
  • Accepting a hire notifies IT, HMO and Payroll automatically and creates the onboarding tasks.
  • Buddies accept or decline by email link. A decline or a 24-hour silence moves to the next eligible person.
  • Employees start their own exit request. HR cannot close an exit until the required checklist is cleared.
  • Google Workspace sign-in limited to named staff, with every action recorded against the real person who took it.
  • Recipient emails and settings change from a Settings page, no developer needed. Bulk import from CSV.

How it was built

React and Vite front end on Vercel. Express 5 API with Drizzle ORM on Neon Postgres, deployed to Render. Shared Zod contracts keep the API and the UI in agreement. Every feature was specified in plain language, generated with AI, then read, tested and corrected by me.
